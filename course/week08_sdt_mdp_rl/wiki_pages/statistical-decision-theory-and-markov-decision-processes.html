In this unit, we start examining how agents ought to take an action and then sequences of actions. For the former, we will cover statistical decision theory. For the latter, we will bring up the mathematical framework of Markov Decision Processes. Algorithms that find solutions for how to act in Markov Decision Processes are commonly known as reinforcement learning algorithms. We will walk through applying Q-learning to a 3x3 grid world setup.
