A bit about Bayesian networks. They were developed in machine learning as tools for minimizing the number of values they need to specify in a joint probability distribution. I will discuss the product rule and how it is used to "breakdown" the joint probability distribution into different dependencies. I will then present the canonical three node networks and how statistical independence works with them. I conclude with an example of using a Bayesian network to simplify computation.
