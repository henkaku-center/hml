Big questions to keep in mind throughout the class:

  1. How does knowledge guide learning, inference, and decision-making from sparse, noisy or ambiguous data?
  2. What are the form and contents of this knowledge?
  3. How is this knowledge itself learned?
  4. How do we balance constraint and flexibility, assimilating new data to our current model versus accommodating our model to the new data?
  5. How can accurate inductive inferences be made efficiently, even with complex hypothesis spaces?

 

One way to think of Computational Cognitive Science is as trying to understand the mind via reverse engineering:

  1. Specify computational problems for a task
  2. Identify ideal solutions to these problems
  3. Test and compare how well these solutions capture human behavior on the task
  4. Compare model performance to state-of-the-art related machine learning models.
