How should we analyze complex systems, such as the human mind? Should we treat it as an adaptive machine that seeks to optimize some function? Or is it best to examine the step-by-step mechanisms or algorithms that it goes through to produce solutions? Or should we focus on the gushy/gooey neural stuff?

Focusing on any one of these questions seems to miss part of what's important about the mind. Further, trying to solve all three simultaneously is too challenging (even solving one of them is too hard for your professor!). 

Although there are many proposed frameworks for investigating different aspects of how systems work, Marr's levels of analysis has been one of the most influential.
