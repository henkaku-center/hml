In this lecture, we’ll cover the basic philosophical principles around the connectionist approach to the mind and the basic definition of artificial neural network models. Then we’ll discuss the linear variant of neural networks – how they work, learning, and their limitations. Then we’ll take a non-linear turn to them and discuss how to train multilayer artificial neural network models with backpropagation. Then we’ll discuss two special case architectures for artificial neural networks that have played a large role in vision and language applications: convolutional and recurrent neural networks, respectively.
