Assignment details

Clusters (7.5%)

Open in Colab: clusters.ipynb

Investigate categorization and prediction in a 2-component Gaussian mixture model. You will derive the posterior and marginal distributions using Bayes' rule and the Law of Total Probability, then implement the model in GenJAX.

  • Prep reading: Textbook — Mixture Models (T3 Ch 5)
  • GenJAX background: Tutorial 2, Chapters 0–4
  • Submit: completed notebook (runs end-to-end) plus derivations for parts (a) and (c).
